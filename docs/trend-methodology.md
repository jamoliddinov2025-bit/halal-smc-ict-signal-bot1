# Trend methodology — Phase 3

## Confirmed evidence only

At the close of candle `t`, retain the latest **two confirmed swing highs** and
latest **two confirmed swing lows**, each in chronological confirmation order.
They are generated by the [strict fractal detector](market-structure-methodology.md)
and have `confirmed_index <= t`. A candidate that has not completed its right
flank cannot participate, even if it looks like an obvious high or low so far.

The two same-kind sequences are independent: swings are not forced to alternate.
No indicator, moving average, regression, volume threshold, or future extrema
selection is involved. Values remain exact `Decimal` comparisons.

## Classification rule

Let `H0, H1` be the older/newer confirmed highs and `L0, L1` the older/newer
confirmed lows:

| Available evidence | Direction | `ready` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fewer than two highs or fewer than two lows | `ranging` | False |
| `H1 > H0` and `L1 > L0` — higher high and higher low | `bullish` | True |
| `H1 < H0` and `L1 < L0` — lower high and lower low | `bearish` | True |
| All other complete pairs: mixed direction or any equality | `ranging` | True |

`ready=False` is **insufficient evidence**, not a claim that price is physically
consolidating. For example, a strictly rising series with no confirmed pullback
swings can remain `ranging, ready=False`. Consumers must check readiness rather
than treating warm-up as a tradable range. The minimum fractal window alone does
not guarantee two highs and two lows.

Equal highs/lows do not meet either strict trend rule. Their labels are not
resolved using later price action. No tolerance, rounded-price bucket, or
asset-dependent threshold is applied in Phase 3.

## Time and state

`TrendState` contains:

- `candle_index`: zero-based processed candle index.
- `timestamp`: that candle's UTC **opening-time identifier**; the state is known
  only after that candle closes.
- `direction`: typed `TrendDirection` (`bullish`, `bearish`, `ranging`).
- `swing_highs` and `swing_lows`: frozen tuples of at most two confirmed `Swing`
  objects, making the underlying evidence auditable.
- `ready`: derived property indicating complete evidence.

The classifier rejects future confirmation indices/timestamps, wrong kinds,
duplicated or unordered evidence, and mutable evidence collections. Public
`classify_trend` accepts trusted confirmed Swing records; it checks their causal
metadata, but cannot prove a manually fabricated Swing matches an unseen candle
history. Use the detector/analyzer to obtain actual validated confirmations.

Between new confirmations the classification stays the same. A newly confirmed
high or low can change it on that confirmation candle, never on the earlier pivot
candle. Every published state and its nested evidence are immutable.

## Relationship to BOS and CHoCH

Structure events use the trend published **through t-1**. The trend state at `t`
is then recomputed after this candle's new swing confirmations are activated.
Thus `StructureEvent.trend_before` and the current snapshot's direction can differ.

A CHoCH is an opposing closing break, **not** an immediate trend reversal. The
swing-derived trend must wait for its own confirmed evidence. This deliberate
separation avoids inventing higher/lower swings to fit a break or later outcome.
Ranging-state breaks are consumed but not classified as BOS/CHoCH; no initial
break bias or trade recommendation is inferred.

## Configuration and replay assumptions

`AnalysisConfig(fractal_length=5)` uses a total five-candle window and two-candle
confirmation delay. `[analysis]` TOML configuration requires exactly that one key;
valid total lengths are odd integers from 3 to 1001. The upper bound limits
buffer/scan size, not a guarantee of enough source history. Configuration is
immutable for an analyzer's lifetime; create a new analyzer for a different length.

Analysis uses observed candles and does not fill data gaps or validate timeframe
cadence. A comparison between runs must use the same canonical prefix, starting
history, and configuration. Fetching a different latest-N history window changes
warm-up/evidence and is not equivalent to continuing an existing analyzer.

See [no-look-ahead guarantees](no-look-ahead.md). No trend strength, trading signal,
entry/exit logic, or excluded Phase 4 feature is implemented here.
